Shenzhen & Guangzhou

3 Nights · 3 Rounds · Mission Hills & Foshan

Mission Hills is the largest golf club on earth — a Guinness World Record twelve championship courses and 216 holes across Shenzhen and Dongguan, signed by Jack Nicklaus, Greg Norman, Sir Nick Faldo, Ernie Els and Annika Sörenstam — under an hour from downtown Shenzhen and within easy reach of Guangzhou and the Hong Kong border. Two of your three rounds are played here; the third belongs to Foshan, tournament home of the European Challenge Tour.

Mission Hills — Two Rounds

Choose any two of the twelve. Most guests open on the World Cup Course, Jack Nicklaus's flagship and stage of the 1995 World Cup of Golf, then take on Greg Norman's — widely rated the sternest test on the property. Faldo, Els, Sörenstam and Ozaki are all a buggy ride away if you'd rather mix it differently.

Foshan Golf Club — One Round

The home of the Foshan Open on the European Challenge Tour — a championship par-72 reshaped by Tony Cashmore, kept in tournament condition year-round and among the first courses in China to earn GEO sustainability certification. Less than an hour from central Guangzhou.

Optional Add-On

Lion Lake, Qingyuan

Swap a round — or stay for a fourth. Rick Jacobson's 7,242-yard Moon Course at Lion Lake runs beneath the red sandstone towers of a UNESCO-listed Danxia landscape, the only course in the world built on one, about an hour north of Guangzhou.

Where You Stay

The resort's own five-star hotel at Mission Hills, a buggy ride from the first tee. We book everything — your room is waiting when you arrive.

Door to Door

A private car collects you from wherever you're staying — your hotel, or straight from the Canton Fair halls at Pazhou — and returns you to either airport or the Hong Kong border. Tee times, caddies and clubs are arranged before you arrive.

You change nothing about your trip. We build the golf around it.

Shanghai + Hangzhou

4 Nights · 2–3 Rounds · Two Cities by Rail

A wild links morning at Yangtze Dunes — China's top-ranked modern course — then the high-speed rail south to Hangzhou for a closing round through the tea terraces of Fuchun. West Lake at dawn before you fly.

The Golf
  • Yangtze Dunes — a true links on the Yangtze estuary
  • Fuchun Golf Club — fairways threaded through a Longjing tea plantation
  • Optional: Sheshan, home of the HSBC Champions
Where You Stay

Your Shanghai hotel works as the starting point — then Fuchun Resort itself, grey-tiled pavilions above the tea terraces, on the course you'll play.

Door to Door

Collected from your hotel or the exhibition halls, rail tickets in hand, met at every station. Your luggage moves with you; your clubs are waiting at each course.
