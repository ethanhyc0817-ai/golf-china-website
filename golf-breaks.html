The Idea

Already in China? The hard part is done.

You've made the flight and the meetings are behind you. What most visitors never discover is that world-class golf — the World Cup's home course, a wild Yangtze links, fairways beneath the Great Wall — sits within reach of every major business city. Give us three to five days and we'll turn the end of your trip into the best part of it.

Choose Your City

Wherever business takes you, golf is close.

Each break is private and door to door — collected from your hotel, delivered back to the airport. These are starting points; every one is shaped to your dates and your pace.

Shenzhen & Guangzhou

The Greater Bay Break

3 Nights · 3 Rounds · On-Resort at Mission Hills

The world's largest golf resort sits between the two cities: twelve championship courses across Mission Hills Shenzhen and Dongguan, led by the World Cup–venue Olazábal Course. Stay on-resort, play a different design every day, and be back at either airport — or the Hong Kong border — the same afternoon.

The golf: Olazábal Course · World Cup Course · a third design of your choosing

Timed around the Canton Fair? We'll collect you straight from Pazhou between phases.

From $1,950 per golfer.Plan This Break
Shanghai + Hangzhou

The Jiangnan Break

4 Nights · 2–3 Rounds · Two Cities by Rail

A wild links morning at Yangtze Dunes — China's top-ranked modern course — then the high-speed rail south for a closing round through the tea terraces of Fuchun, staying at the resort on the course. The Bund is your first evening; West Lake at dawn is your last morning.

The golf: Yangtze Dunes · Fuchun Golf Club · optional Sheshan, home of the HSBC Champions

In town for the CIIE or a trade week? The rail south takes about an hour.

From $2,950 per golfer.Plan This Break
Beijing

The Capital Break

3–4 Nights · 2 Rounds · Golf Beneath the Great Wall

Nicklaus fairways beneath the Great Wall at Reignwood Pine Valley, a closely-held private club, and lakeside golf at Topwin on Yanqi Lake, where President Obama played. One morning stays free — the Forbidden City, privately guided, before your evening flight.

The golf: Reignwood Pine Valley · Topwin at Yanqi Lake

Wrapping up meetings in the capital? Both courses are under ninety minutes from the CBD.
