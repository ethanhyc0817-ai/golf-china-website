Day by Day

The shape of the journey.

Three nights, three rounds — off the plane and onto the first tee, with the most famous course saved for last.

GuangzhouNights 1–2
Day 1 Arrive → Round One

Straight off the plane, onto the red cliffs.

Land at Guangzhou Baiyun and drive straight north — Lion Lake is barely an hour from the airport. Play Rick Jacobson's Moon Course, the only course in the world routed across a UNESCO-listed Danxia landform, red sandstone rising over every hole. Then south into the city: check in on the Pearl River and sit down to a welcome Cantonese dinner.

Golf · Lion Lake, Moon CourseLanding to first tee, about an hour
Day 2 Guangzhou

Old Canton, from yum cha to the Pearl River.

No golf today — Canton's turn. Morning yum cha the way locals take it, bamboo steamers and strong tea, then the Chen Clan Academy and the old Xiguan lanes with your private guide, and the colonial arcades of Shamian Island in the afternoon. As the lights come up: dinner by the river, and Canton Tower burning through the dusk.

Yum cha & old CantonCanton Tower by night
Guangzhou → ShenzhenNight 3
Day 3 Round Two

A Challenge Tour test, then the skyline.

Morning at Foshan Golf Club — a private members' club and home of the European Challenge Tour's Foshan Open, whose eighteenth has staged more than a few closing dramas. Lunch in the clubhouse, then east to Shenzhen. Nothing scheduled tonight: check in at Futian or Shenzhen Bay and let the skyline do the work over dinner and drinks — traditional Canton to modern China, in a single day.

Golf · Foshan Golf ClubShenzhen Bay by night
Day 4 The Finale

Finish where golf in China met the world.

The last morning belongs to the World Cup Course at Mission Hills — Jack Nicklaus's flagship at the Guinness-record largest golf club on earth, and the stage of the 1995 World Cup of Golf. Lunch at the club, then a short drive to Shenzhen airport — or straight across the border into Hong Kong.

The Golf

Three rounds, three different worlds.

Red sandstone cliffs, tournament parkland and the largest golf club on earth — all within an hour of each other.

Round 1 · China Top 10
An Hour North of Guangzhou

Lion Lake — Moon Course

The opening round could not feel less like city golf. Rick Jacobson's 7,242-yard Moon Course is the only course in the world routed across a UNESCO-listed Danxia landform — red sandstone towers rising sheer above the fairways, the lake below. Ranked China Top 10 and Asia Top 100, an hour north of Guangzhou.

Round 2 · Challenge Tour Venue
Foshan · Guangdong

Foshan Golf Club

A private members' club and the home of the Foshan Open on the European Challenge Tour — Tony Cashmore's championship par-72, kept in tournament condition year-round, its eighteenth the stage for more than a few closing dramas. Among the first courses in China to earn GEO sustainability certification; guest play arranged through us.

Round 3 · The Finale
Guanlan · Shenzhen

Mission Hills — World Cup Course

The largest golf club on earth: a Guinness World Record twelve championship courses and 216 holes across Shenzhen and Dongguan, signed by Nicklaus, Norman, Faldo, Els and Sörenstam. You close the journey on Jack Nicklaus's World Cup Course — stage of the 1995 World Cup of Golf, one of the courses that put golf in China on the world stage. Prefer a sterner test? Norman's design is a buggy ride away.
